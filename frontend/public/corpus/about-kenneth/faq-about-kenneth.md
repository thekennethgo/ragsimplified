He is exploring the AI engineering space, and he liked the idea of a visual app that explains how a technology works to people without a technical background. Here, an office of cartoon characters acts out each step of retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) with the real data from your question.

## How did Kenneth build this site?

Step by step with Claude Code, following a written plan: one AI model plans each small step, another implements it, and Kenneth reviews and merges every pull request himself. Lint, tests and an evaluation suite check each change.
