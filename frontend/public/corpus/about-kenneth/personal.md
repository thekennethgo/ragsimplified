# About Kenneth Go

Kenneth Go is a computer science graduate from UNSW Sydney who wants to build software that turns real-world problems into working products, and who is currently exploring AI engineering. He built this site.

## Where Kenneth is from, where he lives and where he has travelled

Kenneth grew up in Batam, Indonesia, and now lives in Sydney, Australia. His most recent trip was to Central Asia.

## Languages

Kenneth speaks English and Indonesian fluently and a little bit of Chinese.

## Education

Kenneth studied a Bachelor of Computer Science at UNSW Sydney from 2023 to September 2026 and graduated with a Distinction average. During his degree he took part in several hackathons and was a subcommittee member of the UNSW Muay Thai Society.

Kenneth enjoys learning most about system design and software architecture.

## How Kenneth got into programming

Kenneth has always been tech-savvy. In middle school he developed a mobile game and deployed it to the Google Play Store, found out he enjoyed programming, and decided then to pursue it as a career.

## Kenneth's view on AI

When AI coding tools first appeared, Kenneth was sceptical: they looked like a lazy way to get tasks done. Once he understood how they work, he saw that using them well makes him far more efficient. He now uses them deliberately, and is learning how AI agents are built.
