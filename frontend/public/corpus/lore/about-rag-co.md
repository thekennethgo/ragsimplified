# About RAG.co

> Fictional. Part of the RAG.co story in this app, not real terms, people or events.

RAG.co is a small company that "looks it up, then writes it down". It was founded on 4 October 2026 at a single desk, and it has been answering questions from its shelves ever since. Its promise is simple: every answer points to a card on a shelf, and when the shelves do not cover a question, RAG.co says so.

## How it started

RAG.co opened on Sunday 4 October 2026 with one desk, one lamp and one very long ladder. The founders had noticed that most answers in the world are given by people who are fairly sure, and most mistakes come from being fairly sure. They decided to build a company that is never fairly sure. It is either holding the card, or it says it is not.

The first week was spent filing. By the end of October the Archive on Level 2 held several hundred cards on subjects from tides to tea, and the Query Desk team was answering questions all day.

## The office

RAG.co is on Level 3 of 12 Index Lane, in the Card Catalogue District. The district is fictional, and so is the address. The building has six rooms.

- **The Query Desk** is the front counter. It opens whenever a question arrives.
- **The Reading Room** is where cards are read and checked against each other. It has eleven chairs and one that is always taken.
- **The Mail Room** receives papers from members and sorts them into folders. A member's papers are never filed in the shared cabinet.
- **The Map Room** holds a wall-sized map of every shelf, with pins where related cards sit close together. Staff say the map is the only place in the building where a stranger can see at a glance what RAG.co knows.
- **The Kitchen** has the coffee machine, called Bean There, which makes 25 cups an hour, and a fridge cleared every Friday.
- **The Archive** is on Level 2. It holds the shelves, the card drawers and the warmest radiator in the building.

## Waking up

RAG.co does not keep a night staff. After a quiet spell the building goes to sleep: the lights dim, the lift stops, and the Query Desk bell is covered with a tea towel. When a question arrives, the building wakes. Waking takes up to one minute, and staff ask visitors to be patient and not to press the bell more than twice.

## The rival across the street

Directly across Index Lane is Guesswork & Co., a firm that answers without looking anything up. Its motto, painted over its door, is "Sounds about right." Guesswork & Co. is fast, confident and frequently wrong, and RAG.co has a standing rule that it never borrows an answer from them. The two firms are polite on the pavement. On sunny days Guesswork & Co. staff wave from their window, and RAG.co staff wave back and then check the weather forecast.

## The sign

On the wall of the Query Desk hangs a sign: "Days since last hallucination: 0". It has read 0 since the day it was hung, 11 October 2026. Guesswork & Co. installed the counter as a housewarming joke. RAG.co kept it as a reminder, and no one has changed the number. A small card under it reads: "It is always today."

## Meow, the office cat

Meow is the office cat. She arrived in the second week of October and chose the Archive's warm shelves as her bed. She sleeps on the third shelf from the window, in a spot the Archive keeper has lined with an old blanket. Meow has no duties, but she is treated as a senior member. Her rules are simple: she is never moved, never woken, and never put on a card. Members of the Coffee Club promise as much in the Meow clause of the club's terms.

## The Coffee Club

The Coffee Club gives members coffee from Bean There, the Kitchen machine. It starts on Monday 9 November 2026. Its terms are set out in their own document.

## People

RAG.co refers to its people by role. The office manager runs the building and the monthly meeting. The facilities team looks after the Kitchen, the radiators and the lift. The Query Desk team answers questions at the counter. The Archive keeper looks after the shelves and Meow. There are also members, who are people who send questions or papers to RAG.co.
