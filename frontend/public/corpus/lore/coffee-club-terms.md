# RAG.co Coffee Club membership terms

> Fictional. Part of the RAG.co story in this app, not real terms, people or events.

*Version 1.0, effective Monday 9 November 2026.*

## 1. Membership

1.1. The Coffee Club gives members coffee from Bean There, the Kitchen machine on Level 3 of 12 Index Lane.

1.2. Membership is open to staff and to members of RAG.co who have signed the visitors' book at least once.

1.3. A membership card is issued at the Query Desk. It is not transferable.

## 2. Fees

2.1. The fee is 12 dollars a month, approved at the monthly meeting of 30 October 2026.

2.2. The fee is paid on the first day of each month, to the office manager.

2.3. A member who joins after the 15th of a month pays half the fee for that month.

2.4. The fee is not refunded for months in which Bean There is out of order for fewer than 5 days.

## 3. Cups

3.1. Each member may have at most 2 cups of coffee a day.

3.2. The limit resets at 08:30. Unused cups do not carry over.

3.3. A cup is a standard Kitchen mug, filled to the line.

3.4. Guests of members are not covered. A member may not hand their card to a guest.

## 4. Cancellation

4.1. A member may cancel with 14 days' written notice to the office manager.

4.2. The fee for the month in which the notice ends is still due.

4.3. A card that is lost is replaced for 2 dollars.

## 5. The Meow clause

5.1. Meow, the office cat, sleeps on the third shelf of the Archive on Level 2 and is treated as a senior member of the building.

5.2. Members may not move Meow, wake Meow, or carry coffee into the Archive for any reason.

5.3. A member who breaks clause 5.2 loses a day of cups, and may be asked to write an apology on a card, which will be filed.

## 6. Machine rules

6.1. Wipe the drip tray after use.

6.2. Report an empty bean hopper to the facilities team. Do not refill it yourself.

6.3. The machine is descaled on the first Monday of each month, and is out of service until 10:00 that day.

## 7. Changes

7.1. These terms are changed only at the monthly meeting, and every change is recorded in the minutes.

## 8. Who runs the club

8.1. The facilities team runs the Coffee Club for the office manager. Questions go to the facilities team lead.

8.2. The fee pays for beans, filters, descaling tablets and servicing of Bean There. It does not pay for milk, which is provided by the office.

8.3. If the fee does not cover the costs in any quarter, the facilities team reports the shortfall at the next monthly meeting and the meeting decides what to do.

## 9. Complaints

9.1. A complaint about the coffee is made to the facilities team lead, in writing, on a card.

9.2. The facilities team replies within 5 working days.

9.3. A complaint that the coffee "sounds about right" but tastes wrong is treated as a serious one.

## 10. Opening and closing times

10.1. The machine is available from 08:30 to 17:30 on working days.

10.2. During the holiday closure the machine is switched off and emptied, and the club fee is not charged for that month.

10.3. The machine does not work while the building is asleep. It takes up to one minute to wake along with everything else.

## 11. Joining

11.1. To join, sign the club book at the Query Desk and pay the first month's fee to the office manager.

11.2. A new member receives a membership card the next working day.

11.3. The club opens on Monday 9 November 2026. Cards are available from Thursday 5 November.
