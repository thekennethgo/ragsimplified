# RAG.co monthly meeting minutes

> Fictional. Part of the RAG.co story in this app, not real terms, people or events.

Minutes of three monthly meetings, kept by the office manager. All meetings are held in the Reading Room at 15:00.

## Meeting of Friday 30 October 2026

**Present:** the office manager (chair), the facilities team lead, the Archive keeper, two members of the Query Desk team.
**Apologies:** one member of the Query Desk team.

1. **Minutes of the previous meeting.** There was no previous meeting. This was the first.
2. **Coffee Club fee.** The facilities team proposed a monthly fee of 12 dollars and a limit of 2 cups a day. After discussion, both were approved by four votes to one. The club opens on Monday 9 November.
3. **Staff handbook.** Version 1.0 was approved to take effect on Monday 2 November.
4. **Fire drills.** The first drill will be Tuesday 3 November at 10:15. Drills will be held on the first Tuesday of each month.
5. **Sign.** It was agreed that the "Days since last hallucination" sign stays at 0.
6. **Any other business.** The Archive keeper asked that no one wake Meow. This was noted.

Meeting closed at 15:50.

## Meeting of Friday 27 November 2026

**Present:** the office manager (chair), the facilities team lead, the Archive keeper, three members of the Query Desk team.
**Apologies:** none.

1. **Minutes of the previous meeting** were approved.
2. **Coffee Club.** 14 members have joined since 9 November. The machine has made 612 cups. The cup limit stays at 2 a day.

4. **Fire drill.** The drill on 3 November took 3 minutes 40 seconds. No repeat was needed.

6. **Any other business.** None.

Meeting closed at 15:35.

## Meeting of Friday 18 December 2026

**Present:** the office manager (chair), the facilities team lead, the Archive keeper, two members of the Query Desk team.
**Apologies:** one member of the Query Desk team.

1. **Minutes of the previous meeting** were approved.

## Standing items

Each meeting follows the same order: the minutes of the previous meeting, the Coffee Club, the Query Desk terms, safety and fire drills, the Kitchen, and any other business. Decisions are taken by a show of hands. The office manager has a casting vote. A motion needs a majority of those present. Anyone on staff may attend and speak, but only the office manager, the facilities team lead, the Archive keeper and the Query Desk team lead may vote.

## Actions carried forward

- Archive keeper: buy a bigger blanket for the third shelf (decided 18 December, up to 15 dollars).
- Office manager: review the Coffee Club fee in March 2027.
- Query Desk team: report any new out-of-scope subjects before the next meeting, so that clause 8.1 of the Query Desk terms can be updated.

## Notes on the vote of 30 October

The Coffee Club fee vote was four to one. The member who voted against said that 12 dollars a month was too much for two cups a day, and asked that the fee be reviewed after three months. The office manager agreed that the fee would be looked at again in March 2027, and this was recorded at the meeting of 18 December.
