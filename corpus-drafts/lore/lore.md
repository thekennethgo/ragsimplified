# RAG.co lore sheet

Source of truth for every RAG.co document. Not seeded, not in `corpus/`. Every RAG.co document must agree with this sheet and must not name any of the app's seven characters or describe how the pipeline works.

Banner line at the top of every RAG.co document: "Fictional. Part of the RAG.co story in this app, not real terms, people or events."

## Company

- RAG.co is a small fictional company that "looks it up, then writes it down".
- Founded 4 October 2026, the day this project started, at a single desk.
- Staff are only "staff", "members" or job titles: the office manager, the facilities team, the Query Desk team, the Archive keeper.
- Rival across the street: Guesswork & Co., which answers without looking anything up. Motto: "Sounds about right." Its opening hours are not stated anywhere.

## Office

- Level 3, 12 Index Lane, in the fictional Card Catalogue District (not a real address).
- Rooms: the Query Desk (front counter), the Reading Room, the Mail Room, the Map Room, the Kitchen, and the Archive on Level 2.
- The Query Desk opens whenever a question arrives. After a quiet spell the building "goes to sleep" and takes up to one minute to wake.
- Kitchen coffee machine: "Bean There".
- Deliberate contradictions (for the conflicting-sources evals, keep them): `about-rag-co.md` says Bean There makes 25 cups an hour, the October newsletter says 40; `about-rag-co.md` says Meow arrived in the second week of October, the newsletter says Monday 26 October.

## The sign

"Days since last hallucination: 0". It has read 0 since it was hung. Guesswork & Co. installed the counter as a housewarming joke on 11 October 2026, and RAG.co kept it as a reminder.

## The cat

Meow, the office cat, sleeps on the Archive's warm shelves.

## Staff handbook facts (v1.0, 1 November 2026)

- Core hours 09:30 to 16:30, Monday to Friday. Office open 08:30 to 18:00.
- Lunch break: 45 minutes between 12:00 and 14:00, taken away from the Query Desk. Lunch is never skipped on a Query Desk day.
- Kitchen: wash your own mug; fridge cleared every Friday at 16:00; no fish heated after 11:00.
- Dress code: smart casual; cardigans encouraged on Level 2.
- Leave: 22 days a year plus the Founding Day holiday (4 October); 5 sick days without a note.
- Fire drills: first Tuesday of each month at 10:15, assembly point the plane tree at the end of Index Lane.
- Visitors sign the visitors' book at the Query Desk and wear a green lanyard.
- Archive quiet rules: whisper only, no food, one cart at a time, never wake Meow.
- When the building goes to sleep: wait one minute, do not press the bell more than twice.
