# Questions about Kenneth Go

Common questions about Kenneth Go, the creator of this site, answered by Kenneth.

## Who is Kenneth Go?

Kenneth Go is a recent computer science graduate from UNSW Sydney. He is pursuing software engineering as a career and he currently lives in Sydney, Australia. He is particularly interested in system design and software architecture.

## What roles is Kenneth looking for?

Graduate or junior roles as a software engineer, AI engineer, full-stack engineer or backend engineer, in Sydney. He is open to remote, hybrid and on-site work.

## What kind of team is Kenneth looking for?

Kenneth is looking for a team that communicates openly and supports each other. Early in his career, he wants to learn as much as he can, so he values colleagues who share knowledge, give honest feedback and help him grow his skills and experience.

## What is Kenneth's strongest skill?

Breaking down real-world problems and finding programming solutions for them, and communicating his ideas clearly to the people he works with.

## What is Kenneth learning right now?

AI agents: how they plan, use tools and work through multi-step tasks.

## What projects is Kenneth most proud of?

Kenneth is most proud of his work as a scrum master and software developer on the university project working with Atlassian. Kenneth also enjoyed developing this site, RAG Visualised, as a way to learn how AI engineering works.

## Why did Kenneth build this site?

He is exploring the AI engineering space, and he liked the idea of a visual app that explains how a technology works to people without a technical background. Here, an office of cartoon characters acts out each step of retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) with the real data from your question.

## How did Kenneth build this site?

Step by step with Claude Code, following a written plan: one AI model plans each small step, another implements it, and Kenneth reviews and merges every pull request himself. Lint, tests and an evaluation suite check each change.

## Does Kenneth have work experience?

Kenneth's main industry experience is an Atlassian client project at UNSW (February to April 2026), where he was Scrum Master and a developer in a team of 5 building an AI-powered Jira prioritisation tool on Atlassian Forge. He also worked as casual wait staff in a restaurant alongside his full-time studies.
