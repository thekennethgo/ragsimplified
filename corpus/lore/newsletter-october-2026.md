# RAG.co newsletter, October 2026

> Fictional. Part of the RAG.co story in this app, not real terms, people or events.

*Published Friday 30 October 2026 by the office manager.*

## Our first month

RAG.co is nearly a month old. We opened on Sunday 4 October 2026 at a single desk, and by the end of the month the Archive on Level 2 holds several hundred cards, the Query Desk has answered questions every day, and the Kitchen has a new machine. Thank you to everyone who brought a question, a mug or a ladder.

## The sign goes up

On Sunday 11 October, a sign was hung on the wall of the Query Desk: "Days since last hallucination: 0". It reads 0 and it will go on reading 0. The counter itself was a housewarming gift from Guesswork & Co., installed as a joke. We decided to keep it as a reminder that the number is always today. The facilities team has taped a small card under it that says so.

## Kitchen upgrade finished

The Kitchen upgrade finished on Friday 23 October. The new coffee machine, Bean There, makes 40 cups an hour, which is four times more than the old kettle-and-jar arrangement. The fridge is also new. It is cleared every Friday at 16:00, and the facilities team reports that last week's clearance found nine unlabelled yoghurts.

## A Guesswork & Co. sighting

On Thursday 22 October a person from Guesswork & Co. was seen on the pavement outside the Query Desk reading our sign for eleven minutes. When asked what they thought, they said it "sounded about right". No further comment was made. We remain on friendly terms and have not borrowed anything from them.

## Meow update

Meow, who first walked in through the Mail Room door on Monday 26 October, has settled on the third shelf from the window. She now has a blanket, donated by the Archive keeper, and a small card that says "Do not file". She has been seen to watch the fire alarm test on 20 October from a distance of four metres. She did not move.

## What is coming

- **Monday 2 November:** the staff handbook, version 1.0, takes effect.
- **Tuesday 3 November, 10:15:** the first monthly fire drill. Meet at the plane tree at the end of Index Lane.
- **Monday 9 November:** the Coffee Club opens. The fee is 12 dollars a month, as approved at the meeting of 30 October. Membership cards are available at the Query Desk from Thursday 5 November.
- **Friday 27 November, 15:00:** the next monthly meeting, in the Reading Room. Everyone is welcome to listen.
- **Tuesday 1 December:** a new version of the Query Desk terms is expected to take effect.

## Reminders

- Lunch is 45 minutes, taken between 12:00 and 14:00, away from the Query Desk.
- Wash your own mug.
- Whisper in the Archive.

## Lost property

One blue scarf, one pair of reading glasses and a single left glove are at the Query Desk. They will be given to the charity shop on 20 November if no one claims them.

## Staff spotlight: the facilities team

The facilities team has had a busy first month. It has fitted three radiators, replaced the Query Desk bell cord twice, found the missing key to the Map Room, and learned that the lift stops when the building goes to sleep. It also counted the chairs in the Reading Room, which has nine, one of which is always taken. The team lead says the best part of the job is the quiet at 06:45, before the building wakes up. The worst part is the yoghurts.

## From the Map Room

The Map Room wall now holds 212 pins. Each pin marks a shelf, and shelves that sit close together are joined with red string. The Archive keeper says the string is for the staff, and the pins are for the cards. If you want to see what RAG.co knows about tides, look in the north-east corner. Tea is in the south-west.

## Numbers for the month

- Days open: 27.
- Questions answered at the Query Desk: more than 300.
- Questions where the answer was "it is not on the shelves": 41, which the Query Desk team counts as a good result.
- Cups of coffee from the old kettle and jar: 1,100 (approximate).
- Number of times the Query Desk bell was pressed more than twice during a wake-up: 9. Please be patient.
- Days since last hallucination: 0.

## A word from the office manager

Thank you for a good first month. We are small and we mean to stay small. Our job is to find the card, read it carefully and tell you what it says. When we cannot find it, we say so. If you see anything in the office that sounds about right but is not on a shelf, tell us.
